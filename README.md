# 📊 Customer Retention Dashboard

An end-to-end churn analytics dashboard built to help a telecom business understand **why customers leave, who is most at risk, and where revenue is being lost** — turning raw customer data into an actionable retention strategy.

![Retention Dashboard Screenshot](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8ff29244-0f7c-4ceb-a172-aeffb071f6ab)

## 🔍 Overview

This project analyzes a dataset of **7,000+ customers** to isolate churn drivers, quantify their financial impact, and give the business a clear roadmap for reducing attrition. The dashboard combines KPI tracking, segmentation analysis, and interactive filtering to translate raw metrics into decisions retention teams can act on.

## ✨ Key Features

- **High-Impact Churn Analysis** — Analyzed 7K+ customer records to isolate a **26.58% churn rate**, identifying **₹2.86M in lost revenue** and pinpointing the critical service failures driving it.
- **Segmented Churn Modeling** — Built churn models segmented by **Contract Type** and **Service Type**, revealing that **Month-to-Month contracts** and **Fiber Optic users** are the highest-risk categories for attrition.
- **Monthly Charges Heat Map** — Implemented a heat map using conditional formatting to visualize the correlation between **customer tenure and billing amount**, providing a data-driven roadmap for loyalty incentives.
- **Interactive Demographic Auditing** — Added multi-select slicers (**Senior Citizen, Internet Service**) enabling deep-dive audits that translate technical metrics into business retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- Data cleaning and preparation of raw customer churn data
- Conditional formatting for heat map visualization
- Multi-select slicers for dynamic, interactive filtering
- KPI cards for churn rate and revenue-at-risk tracking
- Segmentation analysis by contract type, service type, and demographics

## 📈 Key Insights

| Metric | Finding |
|---|---|
| Overall Churn Rate | 26.58% |
| Revenue Lost to Churn | ₹2.86M |
| Highest-Risk Contract | Month-to-Month |
| Highest-Risk Service | Fiber Optic |

## 🎯 Business Impact

The dashboard equips retention and marketing teams to:
- Prioritize outreach to high-risk segments (Month-to-Month, Fiber Optic customers)
- Design targeted loyalty incentives based on tenure and billing patterns
- Quantify the revenue impact of churn to justify retention spend
- Drill into demographic subsegments for more precise, personalized campaigns
